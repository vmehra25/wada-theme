# Source notices

The palette data was extracted from https://colors.elwyn.co/ and independently compared with https://github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations. All 159 hex/name mappings and all 348 combination memberships match; color order follows the website.

Original work: Sanzo Wada, *A Dictionary of Color Combinations*.

Digital dataset: Matt DesLauriers, based on the original open-source compilation by Dain M. Blodorn Kim. Dataset MIT notice: `licenses/dataset-MIT.txt`.

Interactive website: Benjamin Elwyn. Website MIT notice: `licenses/website-MIT.txt`.

The source dataset describes CMYK-to-sRGB conversion and notes possible differences from printed swatches. See https://github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations#cmyk-to-rgb.

The generated mood labels, scores, derived shades and semantic role assignments are contributions of Wada Theme, not assertions about Sanzo Wada's intended categories.
